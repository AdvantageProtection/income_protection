You'll be speaking to Laurence again — the same adviser who prepared your recommendation. He'll run the appointment, take you through the medical questions and submit the application on your behalf.

What happens in this appointment
  1. We confirm your cover. A quick run-through of the providers and options in your recommendation, so you're happy before anything is submitted.
  2. We set up the application. Laurence completes the application with your chosen provider, answering any questions as they come up.
  3. We work through the medical questions. The provider needs medical and lifestyle details from you — this is the bulk of the 40 minutes.
  4. We submit. The application goes in, underwriting begins, and we confirm what happens next and when.
What to have to hand

Helpful, but not essential — we can work around anything you don't have.

  • Your recommendation email, if you'd like to refer to it during the call
  • Your GP surgery name and address — medical questions usually ask for it
  • Your mortgage completion date, if you have it confirmed
  • Details of any existing cover you already hold
How long it takes to be covered

Once submitted, most applications are underwritten within a few days, though some providers may ask for further medical evidence which can add time. We'll keep you updated throughout and chase anything outstanding, with the aim of having cover in place for your mortgage completion.
