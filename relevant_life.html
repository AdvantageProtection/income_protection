What we'll cover

Two of the most valuable — and most commonly missed — protection options for company directors:

Relevant Life Cover

Life insurance provided by your company for you as a director — one of the most tax-efficient ways for a business to protect the people it relies on.

  • Paid for by the company, not from your own pocket
  • Generally treated differently to a personal policy for tax purposes
  • Covers individual directors and employees

Executive Income Protection

Company-funded cover that protects your income if illness or injury prevents you from working.

  • Paid for by the company as a business expense
  • Helps the company continue covering your remuneration while you're off sick
  • Protects both you and the business
